# Klyrow Reputation

Metrics are tenant-scoped and derive volume, delivery, soft/hard bounce, complaint, and failure rates from normalized lifecycle states. Policy states are `GOOD`, `WATCH`, `LIMITED`, and `SUSPENDED`.

`LIMITED` halves provider warm-up caps. `SUSPENDED` denies new submission. The provider executes the state supplied by authorized control-plane policy and does not invent a commercial plan.
